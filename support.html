Famlog Support

A private diary for one family, on iPhone.

Contact

Email iamjunson@gmail.com. Replies usually within a day. Nothing you post is visible to us, so if something looks wrong, a screenshot of the screen in question helps.

Getting started

  • Famlog uses the iCloud account already on your iPhone. There is no Famlog account and no password. Sign in to iCloud in the iPhone Settings first.
  • One person creates the family and invites the others by phone number. Invites arrive by iMessage and only work for that number's Apple Account. The person who invited you can also read you a six-character code to type instead.
  • You and one family member are free. Adding anyone else is a one-time purchase by the family owner, Family unlock, which covers everyone up to 99 people and never renews.

Notifications

  • Famlog needs permission to notify you. If you declined, turn it on under iPhone Settings › Notifications › Famlog.
  • In Famlog, Settings › Notification status shows whether this phone is signed up for alerts from your family, and when it last sent and received one. The Check now button asks the server directly. If something is wrong, the reason is shown there.
  • A VPN on the phone can hold up Apple's notifications. Famlog then checks for new posts by itself now and then, so the alert arrives late rather than never. Turning off the VPN's kill switch usually fixes the delay.

Storage

Your photos and videos are stored in your own iCloud and count against your iCloud plan. If a post can't be saved because iCloud is full, free up space or upgrade iCloud+ under iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud, then tap Retry on the entry.

A post stuck on "Sending…"

The entry itself reaches your family within seconds; the full-size photos follow, and the card says so. If sending stops making progress, Famlog restarts it on its own and, failing that, shows Retry on the card. Your entry is kept on the phone until it has been sent.

Privacy and deleting

  • Everything is encrypted end to end and stored in your family's iCloud. See the privacy policy.
  • Settings › Start over deletes your diary from iCloud and, if you own the family, the whole family. Deleting the app alone removes only the copy on the phone.
