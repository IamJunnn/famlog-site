Famlog Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Famlog is a private diary for one family. It has no Famlog account and no Famlog server. Everything you post is stored in your own iCloud and shared only with the family members you invite.

What we collect

Nothing. Famlog, the developer, does not collect, receive, or have access to any of your data. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software in the app.

Where your data lives

Your entries, photos, videos, comments, names, and reminder times are stored in Apple iCloud, in your family's private database, using Apple's CloudKit service. Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Encryption

Text, names, phone numbers, places, locations, and every detail about an entry (when it was made, how many photos it has, whether it was late, who liked it, reminder times, time zones) are stored in CloudKit's end-to-end encrypted fields. Photos, videos, and profile pictures are encrypted on your device with a key that is itself stored in those encrypted fields. The keys live only in your family's iCloud Keychains. Neither Apple nor the developer can read your content.

What Apple necessarily sees, as the storage provider, is structural: how many records your family has, when they were created or changed, and how large the files are. The developer sees none of it, because developers have no access to users' private iCloud databases.

Phone numbers

To invite someone, you enter their phone number. Famlog asks iCloud which Apple Account that number belongs to and creates an invitation only that account can accept. The number is stored encrypted in your family's iCloud. Invitation codes are stored for seven days in Famlog's public iCloud area and contain only the code and the invitation link, nothing personal.

Location

Adding a place to an entry is off by default. If you turn it on, the city name and coordinates are stored encrypted with that entry and shown only to your family.

Purchases

You and one family member are free. The family unlock, which lets the owner add everyone else, up to 99 people, is a one-time purchase made through Apple's in-app purchase. The developer receives no payment details. Whether the family is unlocked is stored encrypted in your family's iCloud.

Your iCloud storage

Because your photos and videos are stored in your own iCloud, they count against your iCloud storage plan. The app tells you this when you start or join a family, and tells you when a post can't be saved because your iCloud is full.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Silent push notifications from iCloud keep your family's data in sync.

When someone in your family posts or likes your entry, their phone leaves a short note in Famlog's public iCloud area so Apple can deliver the alert. The note contains a code only your family can compute from a key kept in your family's encrypted record, and the alert's details (who, which entry, "shared a photo.") sealed with that same key. It is deleted by the sender's phone after a day. Nobody outside your family can open it or address one to a family member.

Deleting your data

Settings › Start over deletes your diary and, if you are the family owner, the whole family from iCloud, and the local cache from your device. Deleting the app removes the local cache; iCloud data can also be removed under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

Famlog is intended for families. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
